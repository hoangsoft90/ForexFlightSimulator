🚀 Quick Start

Get started in 30 seconds. No account needed — everything stays on your device.

1

Open the app

You'll see your Trader Profile with 4 scores at 50 (fresh start).

2

Tap "PLAY NEXT SESSION"

The app picks a scenario from your current level. Or tap "Choose scenario" to pick one yourself.

3

Read the chart, make a call

You'll see a candlestick chart and a situation description. Choose Buy, Sell, or Wait.

4

See the result + Autopsy

The market plays out. Then you get an honest breakdown: what went wrong, what went right, and your updated scores.

🧠 How It Works

Forex Flight Simulator is a decision-training simulator, not a trading platform. Think of it like a flight simulator — but for trading decisions.

💡 The Core Loop

Observe → Decide → See Result → Learn → Repeat

Every scenario uses real historical price action. You're not predicting the future — you're practicing how to read what the market is showing you.

What makes this different?

  • No real money at risk. You're building instincts, not risking your savings.
  • Honest feedback. The app scores your decision quality, not your profit/loss. A losing trade with good discipline scores higher than a winning trade with bad habits.
  • Rule-based analysis. Every piece of feedback follows a specific rule — no vague "try harder" messages.
  • Progressive difficulty. Start with basics, unlock harder scenarios as you improve.

Decision UI — The Scenario Player

This is where the training happens. You'll see:

  1. Candlestick chart — real historical price action on XAUUSD (Gold) M15 timeframe
  2. Context prompt — a short description of what's happening ("Price rejected at resistance, hammer forming")
  3. 3 action buttons — Buy / Sell / Wait

⚠️ "Wait" is a valid choice!

Sometimes the best trade is no trade. In advanced levels, choosing Wait at the right time earns you bonus Discipline points.

After you tap a button, the chart advances a few more candles to show what happened next. Then you move to the Autopsy.

Trade Autopsy — Learning Moment

This is the most important screen. It shows:

  1. Result badge — Win, Loss, or "Avoided bad setup"
  2. Timeline — key moments (entry, SL hit, result)
  3. Root cause (amber block) — the specific rule that triggered. Examples:
    • "FOMO Entry — entered within 1 min of strong candle"
    • "Emotional SL — moved stop loss after seeing profit"
    • "Late Entry — entered after the setup already played out"
  4. What you did right (green block) — always shown, even on losing trades. This is what keeps you motivated.

⚖️ How Scoring Works

Every decision is scored on 6 components. Your 4 visible scores (Reading, Entry, Risk, Discipline) are averages of these components.

Entry Score

How close your entry was to the optimal zone for that setup. Scored against a pre-defined reference zone — not against hindsight.

Risk Score

How close your risk per trade is to the recommended range (0.5–1%). Higher score = closer to optimal.

RR Score (Risk/Reward)

Whether the trade's potential reward justified the risk taken. Minimum 1:2 RR recommended for most setups.

FOMO Score

Did you enter too quickly after a strong candle? Entering within 1–2 minutes of a big move = FOMO penalty.

Patience Score

Did you wait for the setup to fully form, or did you jump in early before confirmation?

Discipline Score

Did you stick to your plan? No emotional SL moves, no revenge trading, no impulsive entries.

💡 Key Principle

We score your decisions, not your profits. A losing trade with perfect discipline scores higher than a winning trade taken out of FOMO. This is how real traders improve.

💡 Tips for Getting the Most Out of the App

1. Don't rush the "Wait" button

Many beginners never tap Wait. But professional traders say "no" to most setups. Practice patience — it's a skill.

2. Read the Autopsy carefully

The root cause block tells you exactly what rule you broke. Write it down. If you see the same root cause twice — that's your #1 habit to fix.

3. Play 3–5 sessions per day

Short, focused practice beats long grinding sessions. Each scenario takes about 1–2 minutes.

4. Replay levels you've completed

You can always go back to completed scenarios. Retrying with fresh eyes reveals how much you've improved.

5. Check your Discipline score

Discipline is the hardest score to improve but the most important for real trading. If it's lagging, focus on patience and avoiding FOMO.

❓ FAQ

Is this real trading?

No. This is a training simulator. No real trades are executed, no money is at risk, and no broker accounts are connected. All scenarios use historical price data for educational purposes.

Do I need a trading account?

No. You don't need any trading account, brokerage, or financial setup. Just open the app and start practicing.

What markets are covered?

Currently XAUUSD (Gold) on M15 timeframe. More markets and timeframes will be added in future updates.

Why is "Wait" sometimes the right answer?

Professional traders often wait for the right setup rather than forcing a trade. In the app, choosing Wait at the right time earns Discipline points. Waiting is a skill.

Can I change the language?

Yes. Go to Settings (gear icon on Home screen) and switch between English 🇬🇧 and Vietnamese 🇻🇳. All text — including scenario descriptions and feedback — will change.

What does "FOMO Entry" mean?

FOMO = Fear Of Missing Out. It means you entered a trade too quickly after a big candle move, without waiting for confirmation. This is one of the most common mistakes new traders make.

How do I reset my progress?

Go to Settings → Reset All Data. This will clear all scores and completed scenarios. You'll start fresh from Level 1.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. All data is stored on your device. No internet connection is needed to use the app (except for loading ads if enabled).

Is my data private?

Yes. All your trading data, scores, and preferences are stored locally on your device. We have no access to your data. No account or login is required.
