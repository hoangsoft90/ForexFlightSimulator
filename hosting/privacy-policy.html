Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 22, 2026

Forex Flight Simulator ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

The App operates entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers.

  • On-device data: Your trading scores, completed scenarios, language preferences, and app settings are stored locally on your device using device storage.
  • No account required: The App does not require registration, login, or any form of account creation.
  • No personal data collection: We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personally identifiable information.

2. Advertising

The App may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain information to serve personalized ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • IP address (approximate location for ad targeting)
  • App usage data for ad optimization

This data collection is handled entirely by Google AdMob and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. We do not have access to or control over the data collected by AdMob.

You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking → disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"

3. Crash Reporting

The App uses Sentry to collect crash reports and performance data. Sentry may collect:

  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • Crash logs and stack traces
  • App version and performance metrics

No personally identifiable information is included in crash reports. This data is used solely to identify and fix bugs. Sentry's data handling is governed by Sentry's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage & Retention

  • All trading data, scores, and preferences are stored locally on your device only.
  • We have no access to your on-device data.
  • You can delete all app data at any time by clearing the App's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
